The license texts for linked Go dependencies are kept in `licenses/`. The optional validation image contains PHP and Composer under their respective licenses. Composer packages for the Laravel fixture are fetched by the operator and their license identifiers and versions are recorded in its lock file. The application release archives contain the Upgrade Agent executable, examples and project documentation.
